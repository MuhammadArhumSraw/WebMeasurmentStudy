Our Purpose


Our Vision:
Leading the delivery of accurate and precise time to connected devices around the world.

Our Mission: To sustainably cultivate and promote research, educational, and development programs which keep the state of delivered accurate time in step with both the latest advances in technology and the needs of our consumers.

We Support


As a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit, NTF supports enterprise-quality open source software that keeps the world running on time:

  • NTP Project
  • Linux PTP Project
  • libptpmgmt Project
  • Khronos Project
  • Ntimed Project
  • SyncE Projects
  • General Timestamp API
