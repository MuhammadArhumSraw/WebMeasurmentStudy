    Birth of genetic research
    April 25th, 1953: Two Cambridge scientists, Francis Crick and James Watson, publish a paper detailing the structure of DNA, sparking a revolution in molecular biology that paves the way for genetic research. Their discovery will lead to genetic engineering, stem cell research and DNA fingerprinting, and to the mapping of the human genome.
