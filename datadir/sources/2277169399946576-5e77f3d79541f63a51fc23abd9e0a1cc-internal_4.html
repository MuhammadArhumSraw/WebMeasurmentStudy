    How Frontier works

    Get started with the Frontier program

    Access Frontier at work

    If your organization has opted into the Frontier program, you can begin exploring experimental AI features in Microsoft 365.
    • Confirm you have a Microsoft 365 Copilot license
    • Check that your organization has opted into Frontier Contact your admin for details
    • Sign in to Microsoft 365 with your work account
    • Explore Frontier features in supported experiences
    Get started

    Enable Frontier for your organization

    Frontier features are enabled and managed at the tenant level. As an IT admin, you control access to experimental features and agents within your organization.
    • Turn on Frontier for Copilot in the Microsoft 365 admin center
    • Manage agent permissions and feature availability
    • Assign access to eligible users
    Get started

    Try Frontier features with your subscription

    Microsoft 365 Premium, Personal, and Family subscribers can opt in to Frontier directly in supported web apps.
    • Confirm you have an eligible Microsoft 365 subscription
    • Open Word, Excel, or PowerPoint on the web, or open the Microsoft 365 Copilot desktop app
    • Turn on “Preview Frontier features” in Copilot Settings
    Get started
