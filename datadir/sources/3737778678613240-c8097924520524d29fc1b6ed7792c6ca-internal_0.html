Description

ROBLOX – PLAY, CREATE, AND EXPLORE MILLIONS OF EXPERIENCES Join one of the world’s most popular 3D multiplayer platforms where you can play games, create worlds, and connect with a global community of players and creators. Roblox lets you explore an ever-expanding library of user-generated games across genres — from roleplay and simulation to action, adventure, and beyond. Already have an account? Log in and jump into some of Roblox’s most popular experiences — including Grow a Garden, Adopt Me!, Dress to Impress, SpongeBob Tower Defense, Brookhaven RP, and more. WHAT YOU CAN DO ON ROBLOX DISCOVER ENDLESS EXPERIENCES - Explore millions of user-created games and simulations across every genre - Discover trending multiplayer adventures and new releases daily - Join friends in role-playing, building, or competing in team-based challenges CREATE YOUR OWN AVATAR - Customize with clothing, accessories, and animations to make your unique avatar - Browse thousands of user-made avatar items in the Marketplace - Express yourself with custom emotes and styles PLAY TOGETHER—ANYTIME, ANYWHERE - Seamlessly play on mobile, tablet, PC, console, or VR - Enjoy cross-platform multiplayer with friends anywhere in the world CHAT AND CONNECT - Join Parties and hop into experiences together - Users 13+ can chat via voice or text in supported experiences CREATE, BUILD, AND SHARE - Design your own games using Roblox Studio on Windows or Mac - Publish and share your creations with millions of global players SAFE AND TRUSTED ENVIRONMENT - Advanced content moderation powered by human review and AI - Robust parental controls and privacy settings - Community guidelines that promote respect and inclusion - Dedicated Trust & Safety team ensuring a positive experience for everyone WHY MILLIONS PLAY AND CREATE ON ROBLOX - Immersive 3D multiplayer gameplay and community-driven content - Endless creativity — anyone can become a creator - New experiences, events, and genres added daily - A safe, inclusive platform built for imagination and collaboration RESOURCES Create your own experiences: https://www.roblox.com/develop Support: https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us Contact: https://corp.roblox.com/contact/ Privacy Policy: https://www.roblox.com/info/privacy Parent’s Guide: https://corp.roblox.com/parents/ Terms of Use: https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004647846
