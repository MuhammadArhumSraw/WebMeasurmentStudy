    Artificial intelligence is rapidly expanding the enterprise attack surface. It introduces synthetic content, unverified models and autonomous agents acting across critical systems. As AI adoption accelerates, organizations face a new challenge: It is no longer enough to secure these systems; they must be able to prove what can be trusted.
    At the DigiCert Trust Summit 2026, security and technology leaders will explore how trust must evolve from assumption to cryptographic proof. Across content, AI models and autonomous agents, traditional controls are no longer sufficient. Organizations need verifiable identity, tamper-evident integrity and lifecycle governance at scale. At the same time, broader shifts are placing new demands on trust infrastructure. The move toward shorter certificate lifecycles, including the transition to 47-day validity, is accelerating the need for automation and operational resilience. Emerging post-quantum cryptography requirements are redefining how organizations future-proof their security foundations. Together, these changes are reshaping how trust must be established, managed and scaled. 

    This event brings together executive perspectives and real-world practices to address how enterprises can: 

    • Establish verifiable provenance for digital content in an era of generative AI  
    • Ensure integrity and governance across the AI model lifecycle  
    • Deploy strong identity and control for autonomous agents and machine workloads  
    • Adapt to operational shifts like 47-day certificate lifecycles and increasing automation requirements
    • Prepare for the transition to post-quantum cryptography without disrupting the business  

    Join us to understand what is changing, what it means for your organization and how to take the next step toward securing AI and everything it depends on with confidence.
