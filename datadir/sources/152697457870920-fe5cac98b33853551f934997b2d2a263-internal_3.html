FAQs

What are the Meta Quest 3S and Meta Quest 3 storage sizes?

Meta Quest 3S will be available in two storage options: 128GB and 256GB.*
Meta Quest 3 is available in two storage options: 128GB while supplies last, and 512GB.*
*Available storage is less than shown due to existing software. Meta Quest 3 will require certain updates to be installed from time to time, including prior to first use.

Is Meta Quest 3S lighter than Meta Quest 3?

They are close to the same weight. Meta Quest 3S (513 grams) is approximately 2 grams lighter than Meta Quest 3 (515 grams)—roughly equal to a small metal paper clip or pen cap.

Are Meta Quest 3S and Meta Quest 3 wireless?

Yes, Meta Quest 3S and Meta Quest 3 are both all-in-one wireless headsets.

Do I need a Facebook account for Meta Quest 3S and Meta Quest 3?

No, you don’t need a Facebook account to use Meta Quest 3S or Meta Quest 3. You just need a Meta account which you can create with an email address, if you don’t already have one.

Can Meta Quest 3S and Meta Quest 3 play Steam games?

Yes, you can stream the games you love in your Steam library from your computer to both Meta Quest headsets via Steamlink.

What can you use Meta Quest 3S and Meta Quest 3 for?

Gaming
 The biggest titles on the biggest screens. Play your favorite titles on giant 2D screens, or dive into fully immersive action and step into the shoes of your favorite characters.
Entertainment
 Have your own personal theater at home or on the go with Travel Mode. Stream your favorite shows while keeping all your surroundings in view and adjust the screen for size and placement.
Social experiences
 With Quest, you can enjoy popular social apps and multiplayer games, connect with friends from anywhere, host a meeting with colleagues in Meta Horizon Workrooms and more.
Fitness
 Invite a personal trainer into your living room or travel to stunning locations for unreal workouts, with a totally wireless experience.

Do you need a computer for Meta Quest 3S or Meta Quest 3?

No, just download the Meta Horizon App to your smartphone, then wirelessly connect your headset and smartphone to complete the setup process. You can shop top virtual reality titles from the mobile app, or while you’re in the headset.
Get the app

How long is the battery life of Meta Quest 3S and Meta Quest 3?

The Meta Quest 3S battery lasts up to 2.5 hours of usage on average.* The Meta Quest 3 battery lasts up to 2.2 hours of usage on average.*
You can get up to 2 additional hours of headset time and added comfort with the Meta Quest 3 Elite Strap with Battery accessory.
*As with most battery powered devices, battery life is dependent on various factors such as device settings, usage, age of battery, Bluetooth usage, and wireless conditions.

Can you watch movies on Meta Quest 3S and Meta Quest 3?

Yes, with our recently announced Theater Mode you can turn anywhere into your own theater with Meta Quest 3S and Meta Quest 3. Whether you're in your house or on the go, start your movie and watch the room around you automatically darken. Anchor your screen to the perfect placement, and then kick back and enjoy the show. Movie content is available from Netflix and more.

Can you wear glasses with Meta Quest 3S and Meta Quest 3?

Yes. You can wear glasses with the Meta Quest 3S using the glasses spacer included in the box. Custom prescription Meta Quest 3S and Meta Quest 3 lenses are available for added comfort (sold separately).

What is the difference between Meta Quest 3S and Meta Quest 3?

The Meta Quest 3S offers unreal experiences at an unreal price that invites even more people to dive in.
  • Meta Quest 3S storage options: 128GB at $349.99, and 256GB* at $449.99
  • Meta Quest 3S optics quality: High-definition resolution with 1832x1920 pixels per eye
  • Meta Quest headset design: Lightweight comfort
The Meta Quest 3 is our most ultimate Meta Quest experience, with next-level performance** and life-like visuals.
  • Meta Quest 3 storage options: 512GB* at $599.99
  • Meta Quest 3 optics quality: 4K+ Infinite Display, 30% more pixels
  • Meta Quest headset design: Premium slim comfort
**Available storage is less than shown due to existing software. Meta Quest 3 will require certain updates to be installed from time to time, including prior to first use.
**Based on the graphic performance of the Qualcomm Snapdragon XR2 Gen 2 platform vs the Meta Quest 2 platform.
